About Me

I am a PhD student at the Visual Recognition Group, Czech Technical University, advised by Lukas Neumann.

I work on two threads. Label-free 3D detection: training monocular and LiDAR 3D detectors with no human 3D annotations, using temporal consistency and off-the-shelf 2D vision models (TCC-Det, MonoSOWA, MonoSOWA++). Generative 3D reconstruction: diffusion transformers that turn sparse street-level captures into metric building wireframes (WireDiT), the method behind our two S23DR challenge wins.

I build the full stack myself, from data pipelines to multi-GPU training and released code. Before the PhD I spent three years writing production C/C++ firmware, and I completed my MSc with honours and the Dean's Award for my thesis on 3D object detection.

What I Build

Generative 3D reconstruction

Metric building wireframes from a short street-level capture

A few photographs of a house from the street, no drone, become a sparse SfM point cloud with monocular depth and semantics. WireDiT, a flow-matching diffusion transformer, denoises 64 vertex slots into a 3D wireframe while predicting which slots are real corners and how they connect. Scene tokens are anchored to real 3D points, so semantic evidence keeps its metric location; a hull-cropped second pass and a multi-sample consensus sharpen the corners. The output supports roof-area estimation, solar-panel layout, insurance assessment and renovation planning.

  • 0.654 HSS on unseen HoHo houses, state of the art (vertex F1 0.791)
  • 1st place at the CVPR S23DR challenge in 2026 and 2025
  • 68M parameters, trained on 4×H200, one house reconstructed in seconds

WireDiT (WACV 2027) · S23DR 2026 and 2025 winning solutions

Label-free 3D detection

3D object detectors trained without a single human 3D label

An autolabelling pipeline turns unlabelled driving video into 3D boxes: an off-the-shelf 2D instance segmentation model finds objects, temporal consistency across frames aggregates their points, and an outlier-robust template fit recovers metric size and pose. TCC-Det did this for LiDAR, MonoSOWA for a single moving camera by separating object from camera motion and normalising focal length across datasets, and MonoSOWA++ unified both into one fast pipeline with confidence-scored pseudo-labels and pedestrian and cyclist support.

  • 500× faster than the previous state of the art, 1.9 s per frame
  • 25% of KITTI labels plus pseudo-label pre-training beat training on 100%
  • SOTA among annotation-free methods on KITTI and the Waymo Open Dataset

Selected Publications

WACV 2027 · Accepted

WireDiT: A Vertex-Slot Diffusion Transformer for 3D Wireframe Reconstruction

A flow-matching diffusion transformer that reconstructs metric 3D building wireframes from sparse street-level SfM points, depth and semantics by denoising 64 vertex slots conditioned on point-anchored scene tokens. State of the art on HoHo (HSS 0.654, vertex F1 0.791) and Building3D, reconstructing a house in seconds. Paper and code coming soon.

IJCV · Accepted

MonoSOWA++: Universal autolabeling pipeline for Monocular 3D object detection

A universal pipeline for training monocular 3D object detectors without domain-specific human annotations. It supports camera-only and LiDAR weak supervision, aggregates data across different camera setups, and is approximately 500× faster than prior work.

MonoSOWA: Scalable monocular 3D Object detector Without human Annotations

A novel approach enabling 3D object detection from a single RGB camera without human annotations. Introduces a Local Object Motion Model with ~700× speedup over prior work.
